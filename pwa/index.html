Connect Rainette to your PC or Mac.

Vercel hosts only this interface. Search, library access, yt-dlp, and the audio relay run on your computer.

iPhone setup
  1. Run python pwa_companion.py on the computer that has Rainette.
  2. Expose the local port through a trusted HTTPS tunnel.
  3. Open the generated pairing link or scan its QR code.
  4. In Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen.
