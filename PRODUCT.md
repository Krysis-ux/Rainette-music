# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People using Rainette Music as a desktop music app while searching, browsing, queueing, and organizing tracks. They are likely in a focused listening session and need the main window to feel calm, legible, and dependable while the detached player handles transport.

## Product Purpose

Rainette Music provides a standalone music library and discovery surface with ad-free playback, catalog search, playlists, history, queue management, and settings for playback behavior. Success means users can find music, shape the queue, and maintain playlists without fighting the interface.

## Brand Personality

Professional, warm, precise. The app should feel like a polished desktop tool for music, not a copied web page or a crowded prototype.

## Anti-references

Avoid bootleg media-player chrome, cramped icon clusters, generic purple-blue SaaS styling, decorative cards that do not clarify the task, and changes that disturb the detached or docked mini-player.

## Design Principles

- Keep playback control surfaces stable and separate from browsing surfaces.
- Make navigation predictable: primary destinations should be visible without crowding the content.
- Prioritize readable metadata and resilient controls over decorative density.
- Use restrained warmth and consistent component states so the app feels native to daily use.
- Protect the mini-player contract: do not alter its markup, scripts, behavior, or styling unless explicitly requested.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve visible focus states, reduced-motion support, keyboard-reachable buttons, and touch-friendly control sizing where the layout allows.
